#  UdyamiX AI

**UdyamiX AI** is an advanced AI-powered business management system designed to act as a **virtual business manager** for small and medium enterprises. It helps automate and optimize core business operations such as **sales tracking, expense management, inventory control, and AI-based decision making**.

The system combines a **Flutter mobile application**, a **FastAPI backend**, and a **PostgreSQL database**, along with AI modules for intelligent business insights and image-based data processing.

# Application Overview

UdyamiX AI works as a complete business ecosystem:

- **Flutter App (Frontend):** User-friendly mobile interface
- **FastAPI Backend:** High-performance API layer
- **PostgreSQL Database:** Secure and scalable data storage
- **AI Engine:** Smart analytics and business insights

# Key Features

## Smart Business Dashboard
- Live tracking of sales and expenses
- Real-time profit & loss calculation
- Daily, weekly, monthly performance reports
- Business health overview at a glance

## Advanced Analytics & Charts
- Sales vs Expense comparison graphs
- Profit growth trends over time
- Category-wise performance charts
- Inventory movement visualization
- Interactive dashboard (Flutter UI)

## AI-Powered Business Intelligence
UdyamiX AI uses intelligent analysis to:
- Detect best-selling products
- Identify loss-making items
- Predict future stock shortages
- Analyze spending patterns
- Generate automated business insights

Example AI Insight:
> "Your electronics category is generating 35% higher profit this month. Consider increasing stock levels."

## Smart Inventory Management
- Add / update / delete products
- Real-time stock tracking
- Low-stock alerts
- Automatic restock recommendations
- Product-wise sales history

## Image-Based Data Entry
- Upload invoices, bills, and receipts
- Upload product images
- AI extracts:
  - Product name
  - Quantity
  - Price
  - Category
- Automatically stores structured data in PostgreSQL

## Sales & Expense Management
- Record daily transactions
- Categorize expenses (transport, purchase, salary, etc.)
- Auto profit calculation
- Historical financial tracking

## AI Report Generator
- Weekly business reports
- Monthly profit summaries
- Inventory analysis reports
- Exportable structured reports (PDF-ready)
